Stories & Drinks @ IDFA

Join us for an evening of stories and drinks at the International Documentary Film Festival Amsterdam (IDFA) 2024.

  • 📅 20 November 2024
  • 🕰 7-9:30 PM CET
  • 📍 Eye Bar & Restaurant

It's time for one of the documentary highlights of the year: IDFA 🎉 which brings many of us to Amsterdam for the week 🤗 Let's take this opportunity to catch up and meet new friends!

🎥 At 21:30 there is a special screening of the mind blowing film About a Hero by SU member Piotr Winiewicz, which has been selected as this year's opening film 🤩 Get your tickets via IDFA's website 🎟️
